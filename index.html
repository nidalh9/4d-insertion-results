About the moving views

  • Each moving clip starts and ends in the TTM view, and the camera always looks at the object.
  • The size of each move is the largest that stayed inside the reconstructed scene on our checks: scene coverage per frame, the object kept in frame, and a by-eye look at sampled frames for floaters, holes and smears.
  • In most clips, scene splats closer to the camera than a fixed fraction of its distance to the object (0.3–0.6, set per clip) are not drawn. That fraction is set so that the view from the TTM camera does not change.
  • Rows with only the TTM camera have no moving clip that stayed clean, even at a very small move: the captured scene itself breaks up near that view.
  • Each object is reconstructed from one generated video, so a wide move also shows sides that video never saw, where the shape is weakest.
