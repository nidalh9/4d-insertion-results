About the camera moves

  • The camera always looks at the object. Each move is a new render of the same placed object; nothing is fitted again for it.
  • The size of each move is the largest that stayed inside the reconstructed scene on our checks: scene coverage per frame, the object kept in frame, and a by-eye look at the frames for floaters, holes and smears.
  • In most clips, scene splats closer to the camera than a fixed fraction of its distance to the object (0.2–0.6, set per clip) are not drawn.
  • Each object is reconstructed from one generated video, so a wide move also shows sides that video never saw, where the shape is weakest.
